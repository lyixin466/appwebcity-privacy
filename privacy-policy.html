2. Information We Collect

When you use the App, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Email address (via email registration or sign-in)
  • Name and profile photo (with your authorization via Google, Facebook, or TikTok Login)
  • Unique user identifier (UID)

Usage Data

  • Apps you add and the categories and spaces you assign them to
  • App names, links, package identifiers, and custom URL schemes
  • Favorites, ordering, usage counters, and related app-library preferences

Device Information

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version number
  • Crash reports, analytics, and diagnostic data used to improve app stability
  • Advertising ID, where available, after consent to analytics collection where required by applicable law

Authentication Tokens

  • Encrypted tokens used to maintain your login session. We do not store your password in plain text.

Installed App Information

To help you organize apps, AppWebCity can, at your request, read the names, package names, and icons of apps installed on your device for display and categorization. This information is used only on this device and is not uploaded to any server. This refers to the raw list of installed apps. Entries you actively add to AppWebCity (including their name, package name or web address) sync with your account once you sign in; nothing syncs if you are not signed in.

Clipboard

When you return to the home screen, AppWebCity checks whether the clipboard contains a web address, so it can offer to save it as a bookmark. This happens only while the app is in the foreground, the clipboard content is never uploaded, and we only keep the last address we offered so that we do not ask about the same link twice. You can ignore the prompt; nothing is saved unless you tap it.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Docs Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment. We never receive or store your card number, bank details, or billing address.

When you subscribe, the App gives Google a randomly generated identifier for your account, so that the purchase can be matched back to you. It is a random value, not your name or your email address.

For each subscription our servers store:

  • the Google Play purchase token and the product it belongs to
  • which account the purchase is bound to, and the random identifier described above
  • the subscription state (active, cancelled, in a billing grace period, on hold, expired, or revoked), when the current period ends, whether it will renew, and when your membership began
  • whether the purchase was made in Google's test environment

Google notifies us whenever a subscription renews, is cancelled, expires, or is refunded, so that your access stays correct without any action from you. We keep a record of those notifications. We also ask Google for the current state of your subscription when you restore or refresh a purchase.

A purchase is bound to the first account that registers it, and that binding does not move to another account. This is what stops a single subscription from being shared across accounts.

If you choose to tell us why you are cancelling, the reason you select and the number of times you have sent feedback are stored with your account. This is optional, and a subscription can be cancelled without it.

We use this information only to grant and maintain Docs Pro access, to keep it in sync with Google, and to help you if a purchase goes wrong. When you delete your account, all of these records are deleted.

剪贴板

回到首页时,AppWebCity 会检查剪贴板里是否是一个网址,以便询问你要不要收藏。这只在应用处于前台时发生,剪贴板内容不会上传;我们只记住上次询问过的那个网址,避免对同一个链接反复打扰。你可以忽略该提示,不点击就什么都不会保存。

购买与订阅

Docs Pro 是可选的自动续订订阅,通过 Google Play 销售。款项由 Google 处理,我们不会收到或存储你的卡号、银行信息或账单地址。

订阅时,本应用会把一个随机生成的账号标识提供给 Google,以便把这笔购买对应回你的账号。它是一个随机值,不是你的姓名,也不是你的邮箱地址。

对每一笔订阅,我们的服务器会存储:

  • Google Play 的购买令牌,以及它所属的商品
  • 这笔购买绑定到哪个账号,以及上面所说的随机标识
  • 订阅状态(有效、已取消、处于付款宽限期、账号保留、已过期或已撤销)、当前周期的结束时间、是否会续订,以及你成为会员的时间
  • 这笔购买是否发生在 Google 的测试环境中

当订阅续订、被取消、到期或被退款时,Google 会通知我们,使你的权益无需任何操作即可保持正确。我们会保留这些通知的记录。当你恢复购买或刷新状态时,我们也会向 Google 查询订阅的当前状态。

一笔购买绑定到首个登记它的账号,该绑定不会转移到其它账号。正是这一点使得一个订阅无法在多个账号之间共用。

如果你选择告诉我们取消的原因,你所选的原因以及你提交反馈的次数会与你的账号一同存储。这是可选的,不提交也可以取消订阅。

我们仅将这些信息用于授予和维持 Docs Pro 权益、与 Google 保持同步,以及在购买出问题时为你提供帮助。当你删除账号时,上述记录会被全部删除。
