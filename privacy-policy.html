2. Information handled by AppWebCity

Account information. If you create or use an account, we may process your email address, display name, profile photo, a Firebase user identifier, and authentication tokens. Depending on platform and app version, sign-in options may include email, Google, Facebook, or Apple.

App-library information. We process the apps and services you add, including their names, links, package identifiers or URL schemes, categories, custom spaces, favorites, ordering, and usage counters. When you are signed in, this app-library information may be synchronized through Firebase so it is available on your other devices.

Search information. Search terms may be sent to the Apple iTunes Search API to return app names, icons, and related metadata. Please avoid entering personal or sensitive information as a search term.

Analytics and technical information. Firebase Analytics may process app interactions, app-instance or device identifiers (including the Advertising ID, where available and, where required by applicable law, after you consent to analytics collection), device type, operating-system version, app version, and related technical information. We use this information to understand feature usage and improve reliability. AppWebCity does not display third-party advertising and does not sell personal information.

Local notes, documents, and attachments

Notes and documents you create, together with photos, PDFs, scanned pages, and annotated images you select or generate, are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. AppWebCity does not upload this content to Firebase or our servers and does not use it for advertising or analytics.

3. Device-only and user-controlled access

  • Installed apps on Android: At your request, AppWebCity can read the names, package names, and icons of launchable apps installed on your device. The complete installed-app list stays on your device. Apps you choose to add become part of your app library and may be synchronized when you are signed in.
  • Photos and files: AppWebCity uses system pickers so you choose the specific photo, image, or PDF the App may access.
  • Document scanning: Scanning is provided by Google Play services and is processed on-device. AppWebCity receives only the pages you confirm and return from the scanner.
  • Voice input: If you choose voice input, AppWebCity requests microphone access and passes speech to the speech-recognition service selected on your device. AppWebCity does not retain the raw audio recording. Your speech-recognition provider may process audio under its own privacy terms.

Depending on your operating-system and device settings, app-private local content may be included in a device backup or restored during device migration. Those backups are controlled by your operating-system or backup provider, not by AppWebCity.

4. How we use information

  • Provide app organization, custom spaces, notes, documents, search, and related features.
  • Authenticate users, protect accounts, and send sign-in or password-reset messages.
  • Synchronize signed-in users’ app libraries between devices.
  • Understand feature usage, troubleshoot problems, and improve AppWebCity.
  • Prevent abuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Retention and account deletion

Account information and synchronized app-library information are retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can permanently delete your account and associated cloud app-library data from the AppWebCity profile/settings screen by selecting Delete account. You may also email us at bangruifang@gmail.com for assistance; we aim to respond to verified requests within 30 business days.

Local notes, documents, and attachments are not part of your Firebase account. You can delete them in the App. Uninstalling AppWebCity removes its app-private local storage, subject to any device backup or restore settings controlled by your operating system.

2. AppWebCity 处理的信息

账户信息：当您创建或使用账户时，我们可能会处理您的电子邮箱、显示名称、头像、Firebase 用户标识符以及身份验证令牌。根据平台和应用版本，登录方式可能包括邮箱、Google、Facebook 或 Apple。

App 库信息：我们会处理您主动添加的 App 和服务，包括名称、链接、应用包名或 URL Scheme、分类、自定义空间、收藏状态、排序和使用次数。当您登录账户后，这些 App 库信息可能通过 Firebase 同步，以便在您的其他设备上使用。

搜索信息：搜索词可能会发送至 Apple iTunes Search API，用于返回 App 名称、图标及相关元数据。请不要在搜索框中输入个人信息或敏感信息。

分析与技术信息：Firebase Analytics 可能会处理应用交互、应用实例或设备标识符（包括设备提供且在适用法律要求同意时仅在您同意分析收集后使用的广告 ID）、设备类型、操作系统版本、应用版本及相关技术信息。我们使用这些信息了解功能使用情况并改善稳定性。AppWebCity 不展示第三方广告，也不出售个人信息。

本地备忘录、文档与附件

您创建的备忘录和文档，以及您主动选择或生成的照片、PDF、扫描页和标注图片，保存在设备的应用专属存储中。AppWebCity 不会将这些内容上传至 Firebase 或我们的服务器，也不会将其用于广告或分析。

3. 仅在设备端或由用户控制的访问

  • Android 已安装 App：在您主动操作时，AppWebCity 可以读取设备上可启动 App 的名称、包名和图标。完整的已安装 App 列表仅保留在设备上；您主动添加的 App 会成为 App 库的一部分，并可能在登录后同步。
  • 照片与文件：AppWebCity 使用系统选择器，由您决定允许访问的具体照片、图片或 PDF。
  • 文档扫描：扫描功能由 Google Play 服务提供并在设备端处理。AppWebCity 只接收您确认并从扫描器返回的页面。
  • 语音输入：当您主动使用语音输入时，AppWebCity 会请求麦克风权限，并把语音交给设备上选定的语音识别服务。AppWebCity 不保留原始录音；语音识别服务提供方可能依据其隐私条款处理音频。

根据操作系统和设备设置，应用专属的本地内容可能包含在设备备份中，或在更换设备时恢复。这类备份由操作系统或备份服务提供方控制，并非由 AppWebCity 控制。
